# Barbarian Path: Path of the Ancients

*A barbarian's rage is often personal—an eruption of lived pain and present fury. But those who walk the Path of the Ancients rage with a breath not entirely their own. Their fury is inherited, shaped by voices that linger beyond death. There is purpose in that connection—not devotion, but utility. They do not revere the dead; they command them. And when they fall, it is unclear whether the spirit that departs was ever singular to begin with or if it was simply the last echo in a chorus of ancestors unwilling to let go.*

In the primeval forests where the fabric of reality is thin and the spirits of the first warriors roam, the Path of the Ancients beckons the brave and the bold. These warriors bond with the ancestral spirits, not for protection but for conquest, their essence woven with whispers of primordial battles. Each of these chosen champions carries the echoes of ancient wars, their arms guided by spectral hands thirsty for glory. As they stride into battle, the air shimmers with the might of the forgotten ones, and their cries of valor resound through the echoes of time. To walk this path is to carry the mantle of epochs past and to unleash the wrath of history's fiercest souls upon any foe that dares to challenge the old blood.

#### Song of the Old Blood

*3rd-Level Path of the Ancients Feature*

You gain the ability to perceive and communicate with the spirits of your ancestors. This connection provides access to their guidance and wisdom. Once you use either option, you can't use that option again until you finish a long rest.

- **Spiritual Insight :** You can seek guidance from your ancestors for advice on matters related to history, lore, and relevant traditions. You can perform a 10-minute ritual to ask the spirits a single question. The GM will provide an answer based on the knowledge and perspective of your ancestors. This insight does not predict the future but may offer wisdom, warnings, or insight based on past experiences.
- **Ancestral Language :** Your connection to your ancestors grants you the ability to comprehend ancient languages. You undertake a 1-minute ritual, after which you gain the benefits of the *comprehend languages* spell. This ability reflects the ancestral spirits imparting their knowledge upon you, allowing you to understand spoken words and decipher written messages in any language, as if the voices of the past are whispering these secrets in your ear.

#### Ancestral Fury

*3rd-Level Path of the Ancients Feature*

As you tap into the raw energy of your rage, ethereal forms of your ancestors begin to manifest around you. These ghostly figures, clad in ancient armor and wielding spectral weapons, hover and swirl in a haunting dance of war. Their eyes blaze with the fury of battles long past, and their ghostly voices echo with the cries of ancient war chants, instilling a sense of awe and dread in all who witness this display.

Once per turn while raging, you can deal extra necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus when you hit with a melee weapon attack.

Additionally, as a bonus action after you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can summon a spectral ancestor to strike a different creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The ancestor makes a melee spell attack against the target with an attack bonus equal to Strength modifier + your proficiency bonus. On a hit, the target takes necrotic damage equal to your barbarian level. You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your Strength modifier (minimum of once), regaining all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

#### Soul Reaver

*6th-Level Path of the Ancients Feature*

Your rage taps into a primal force, enabling you to unleash vengeful spirits upon your enemies. If you are raging and an ally you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage from a creature, you can use your reaction to unleash an ancestral spirit at the attacking creature. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw with a DC equal to 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier. A creature takes 2d6 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. This damage increases to 3d6 at 10th level and to 4d6 at 14th level.

#### Spiritual Convergence

*10th-level Path of the Ancients Feature*

When you enter your rage, you forge a spectral bond between yourself and your allies. A number of creatures of your choice up to your Constitution modifier (minimum of one) within 30 feet of you become spirit-linked for the duration of your rage.

Once on each of a linked creature's turns, when it hits with a weapon attack, it can deal extra necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus. In addition, when a linked creature takes damage and is within 30 feet of you, you can reduce that damage by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier (no action required). You can reduce damage this way a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of one) per rage.

#### Wrath of the Wraiths

*14th-Level Path of the Ancients Feature*

You can harness the vengeful power of a thousand raging ancestral spirits. As an action once per rage, you invoke these wraiths to unleash their wrath in one of the following ways. The saving throw DC for these effects is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier.

- **Flames of Vengeance :** The flaming ghosts of your ancestors erupt from your core, engulfing a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on you, passing through objects as if ethereal. All creatures of your choice that you can see within this inferno must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
- **Chain of Wraiths :** A chain of tormented spirits erupts from your outstretched hands in a line 30 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or become restrained by the spirits for 1 minute. Restrained creatures can use an action to repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on themselves on a success.
- **Chill of the Grave :** You unleash a bone-chilling gust of otherworldly cold in a 30-foot cone. Each creature caught in this maelstrom must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 8d6 cold damage on a failed save, and half as much damage on a successful one. Success or failure, each affected creature's speed is halved until the end of your next turn as the grip of the grave hinders their vitality.
